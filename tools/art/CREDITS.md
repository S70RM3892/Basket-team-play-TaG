# Credits / third-party assets

No third-party assets are used. Every mesh, material and sound in `game/Content/Art` and `game/Content/Audio`
is generated by the scripts in `tools/`:

| What | Made by |
|---|---|
| Hero, ball, court, rooftop props, city and sea meshes | `tools/blender/*.py` (Blender 5.2, bmesh, scripted) |
| Materials and material instances | `tools/import/import_all.py` (Unreal 5.8 Python) with colours from `tools/blender/palette.py` |
| Sound effects and the ambient loop | `tools/audio/synth_sfx.py` (Python + numpy, synthesised) |
| Fonts in the preview renders | Blender's built-in font (preview images only) |

If CC0 assets are added later, list them here with the source URL and the licence.
